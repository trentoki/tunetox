Bounded WAV / AIFF streaming — isolated experiment

Four aligned header-detected files: RIFF/WAVE mono/stereo integer PCM (8/16/24/32-bit) or IEEE float32, including standard extensible variants. All must match the context rate and frame count. Pickers show all files so Safari can select .wav/.wave regardless of MIME type; actual audio headers and encoding are checked on Load headers. AIFF and AIFF-C NONE/sowt integer PCM and fl32/FL32 are also supported. No RF64, RIFX, compressed codecs, float64 or resampling. Voice starts dormant. No handoff or existing application integration. Lower device volume before listening.

250 ms chunks; a small start window is prepared asynchronously while stopped/paused. Ready Play reuses it. The provisional 10 ms scheduling lead has a selectable 50 ms fallback. Activation fades over 10 ms once ready. Preparation, scheduling lead and fade are separate delays. Predicted onset timing is not measured audible latency.
