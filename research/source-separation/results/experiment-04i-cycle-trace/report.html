Experiment 4I — why ordinary ramps disappear

They are usually not lost in rendering. The trace finds detected cycles with correctly generated signatures that fail fixed target-confidence checks, so the renderer intentionally writes no audio there. The original ramp then remains in the residual.

Frozen 4H baseline · diagnostic only · no audio exports, recognition changes, new synthesis, linear256 combination, MIDI or 4J.

Direct findings

  • 102 significant flat-output intervals are traced in the CSV/JSON. None intersects declared accepted support. All have original waveform energy and residual samples identical to original.
  • 70 isolated rejected cycles have accurately rendered neighbors within 0.1 period on both sides. Of these, 57 fail target shape, 8 fail neighbor checks and 5 fail target periodicity confidence.
  • No accepted-but-unrendered cycle, no missing reliable-cycle signature, and no output outside declared support was found. All 2,013 accepted cycles have nonzero modeled energy.
  • All regenerated reliable rejected-cycle signatures match the saved signatures exactly (maximum difference 0). This rules out a missing/corrupted-signature path for these gaps.

The 102 significant intervals have these dominant causes: 75 target-shape, 9 detection/reliability, 8 neighbor, 7 target-periodicity, 2 partial-boundary, and 1 unsupported-relationship. Intervals may contain more than one candidate; their full mixed-cause energy traces remain in JSON.

Boundary accuracy and the isolated ordinary cycles

Detected boundaries are rising mean-level crossings. Sharp resets are internal cycle landmarks, so their approximately half-period displacement from the boundary is intentional. Compare intervals and relative phase, not absolute coincidence of the two landmarks.

For the 57 isolated target-shape failures, median stored score is 0.994895, narrowly below the frozen 0.995 target requirement. A diagnostic ±0.5-position comparison moves 57/57 above 0.995; every best adjustment in this subset is ±0.25 of a 64-position slot. Median score becomes 0.996481. Neighbor tolerance in 4H never applied to this target-side test, which explains why these cycles still disappear.

Independently remeasuring each rejected cycle with both original-audio boundaries translated together by −1, −0.5, 0, +0.5 or +1 sample moves 40/57 isolated target failures above the gate. Across all 93 exclusive target-shape failures, the descriptor probe passes 91 and the native translation probe passes 68. Period duration is held fixed during these probes. Median isolated target period error is 0.134%; median source reset-to-reset versus crossing-period difference in reliable same-period context is 0.272%. These do not indicate a gross missing/misplaced period.

Confirmed: tiny phase/sampling changes can flip a hard confidence decision while the audio remains periodic. Not proved: that every saved boundary is physically wrong, or every rejected cycle should be accepted. Descriptor rotation linearly smooths sampled shapes; translated audio signatures change interpolation, mean and RMS estimates. Genuine shape/phase changes and 64-position sampling sensitivity remain confounded. No probe result was applied to recognition or audio.

Detection versus rejection versus rendering

The 119 omitted reliable ambiguous cycles partition into 93 target-shape, 11 target-periodicity, 13 neighbor, and 2 unsupported-relationship cases. Another shape-low reason overlaps an unsupported pair, hence 94 cycles carry that reason in the original logs but only 93 belong to the exclusive target-shape category here. Each gap’s owner IDs link to its full detection, signature, historical exemplar scores, 4F-B/4H support, rejection and actual output-energy records in report.json.

Unreliable detection candidates already existed but were screened out before normal recognition. This is different from never finding a candidate. Missing support at 0–0.001905 s and 5.997120–6 s lies outside the first/last complete candidate intervals. No rendering omission after acceptance was found. The trace does not reinterpret confidence as ground truth or force release/transition material into a family.

Repeated spikes are not missing ramps

4H has 1616 detected narrow residual events entirely within rendered support. With a fixed 20%-of-original-peak threshold and width ≤15% of the local observed period, median width is one sample (22.7 microseconds), median phase is 0.5123, and median distance to the original sharpest edge is one sample. Zero occur in the bridge phase zone. Their energy is 5.69% of total residual energy.

These spikes are consistent with sharp-edge approximation and sub-sample/high-harmonic phase mismatch in the frozen 64-position model, not erased whole periods or large placement shifts. A sampled maximum-slope location is only a reset proxy, not a physical discontinuity timestamp. This trace cannot uniquely split sharp-edge bandwidth loss from very small phase errors. Entire missing ramps have zero reconstructed support and original-like residual over much of a period; they are classified separately.

What this supports next — no correction implemented

The most direct future controlled test would address bounded target-side alignment sensitivity while keeping strict evidence for different shapes and retaining original timing/phase in any synthesis. 4H changed only neighbors. The data does not justify simply lowering 0.995, automatically merging families, accepting all 119 cycles, or treating every sharp edge as a new cycle boundary. Periodicity and detection failures need separate evidence. No 4J or revised render was started.
