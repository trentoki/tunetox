Smallest evidence-supported next experiment — not implemented

  1. Target the neighbor evidence test first: inspect nearest reliable neighbors in a tightly bounded time/period neighborhood, and compare neighbor geometry with a bounded phase-tolerance probe, rather than requiring immediately adjacent candidate indices at a fixed phase. Keep the target’s own confidence gates, family relationship policy, original timing/phase, and uncertain intervals intact. Do not render skipped candidate intervals or merge family identities. This addresses the measured micro-crossing/phase sensitivity; the 64/70 comparison is an upper diagnostic opportunity, not a promised gain.
  2. Leave genuinely weak target matches unresolved initially. The 55 score-only failures need separate phase/shape/error calibration, not a blind relaxation of 0.995. Negative controls must include genuinely different shapes, polarity, pitch changes, unfamiliar low-level periodic sounds, and real transients.
  3. Separate core exemplars from articulation/edge variants in a later grouping study. Require stable evidence before a release/transition exemplar becomes an equally authoritative global competitor. Preserve variants and unfamiliar shapes rather than force one family. This is separate from local continuity and not a reason to combine A+B.
  4. Treat edge-detail fidelity separately. The narrow internal spikes require improved phase-consistent rich synthesis; bridge/gap fixes will not restore omitted upper detail. 4F already shows that matching high-frequency energy can worsen its phase-sensitive error. No interpolation, resolution or bridge change was made here.

Cost estimate, not a benchmark: a bounded lookup over a few nearby cycles is O(cycles), and five 64-position neighbor comparisons add only a few times the compact comparison work (4F-B preparation was 0.027 s). A conservative future implementation should stay far below exhaustive waveform fitting on this clip, but requires fresh timing/memory and false-match tests. No performance or continuity guarantee follows from this diagnostic.
