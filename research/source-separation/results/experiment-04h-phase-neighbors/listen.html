Precisely what changed

Only the neighbor test for originally ambiguous cycles still omitted by 4F-B changes. The frozen 4F-B chooser supplies all target and competing-family requirements unchanged. Each side uses the nearest reliable temporal neighbor, with a maximum gap of 0.1 target period and the existing 2% duration-change limit. Neighbor cosine must still reach 0.995, but five comparison offsets are permitted: −0.5, −0.25, 0, +0.25 and +0.5 of a 64-position slot (at most 1/128 cycle).

Comparison shifts are never applied to synthesis. Saved family exemplars, cycle duration, start time, amplitude scale, channel offsets and 4D/4F-B linear interpolation/Hermite joins remain unchanged. Skipped unreliable intervals are not filled. All 4F-B rendered cycles and render-family choices remain. Family identity stays unresolved for newly supported ambiguous occurrences.

Of the 75 additions, 64 use a nearby reliable neighbor beyond a tiny rejected crossing interval. The remaining 11 benefit from bounded-phase comparison without such a skipped interval. Different shapes, inverted polarity, unsupported relationships, genuine time gaps, duration transitions and weak target evidence remain rejected in the synthetic tests. The original 399 unreliable candidate intervals and four provisional occurrences remain outside this experiment.

Quality and artifact checks

  • New-cycle error/input energy: 1.511%; worst individual added cycle: 1.876%. No large energetic-cycle error over 25% was found.
  • Added-cycle model/original RMS ratios range 0.971–0.986; median 0.977. No per-cycle peak or gain correction was applied after rendering.
  • Maximum adjacent-sample step at the same evaluated cycle boundaries is unchanged at 0.005638. Median boundary step rises from 0.002389 to 0.002664, with more modeled audio present.
  • Maximum whole-waveform step rises slightly from 0.207758 to 0.209021 (about 0.61%). A sample-step statistic cannot classify all audible clicks or modulation.
  • Exported reconstruction + residual maximum sample difference from original is 7.45e-09. This is subtraction/export conservation, not reconstruction quality.

Listening is still required. No new listening or device playback test was performed. The 64-position upper-harmonic loss remains intentionally unchanged. Fractional descriptor comparison also slightly smooths its samples, so acceptance does not prove exact physical phase error or common sound origin. Eight synthetic and two saved-export tests passed; they do not prove zero false estimates on unfamiliar recordings.

Scope and records

All earlier source, results and audio are preserved. No 4F-A 256-position/cubic/harmonic sampler was used, no A+B render exists, and no 4I was started. No MIDI, isolated stems, scale or instrument identity entered recognition.
