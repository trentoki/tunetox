A: bounded target recognition

Keep the historical best exemplar and the 0.995 threshold. Compare its shape at −0.5, −0.25, 0, +0.25 and +0.5 of a 64-position slot. All other 4H target, phase, harmonic, periodicity, family-relationship and neighbor gates remain unchanged. Comparison shifts are not applied to rendering. No cycle boundaries or discovery assignments are edited.

A recovers all 57 isolated target-score failures highlighted by 4I, plus 32 other supported cycles. It does not infer missing periods or borrow B’s sequence logic. Phase shifting interpolates/smooths descriptors slightly, so a better score is not proof of a physical boundary error or common origin. Remaining weak or incompatible targets stay unexplained.

B: short continuous phase/amplitude predictions

Use only frozen 4H accepted cycles, without A’s added recognition. A proposal requires three stable context cycles on each side, coherent signed geometry, compatible separate family labels, ≤2% period variation, and flank amplitude ratio ≤1.2. A learned exemplar must dominate at least four of the six context occurrences. The missing span must close in one or two predicted periods, within 2% of the neighboring period scale. No leading/trailing extrapolation, long silence or unsupported transition is filled.

Predict the phase grid between observed anchors and interpolate neighboring amplitude/offset metadata. The missing waveform’s signature or scale is not copied. Feed these explicit inferred occurrences through the exact frozen 64-position renderer. Each gap receives only one fixed prediction: no phase/gain search against original and no minimization over alternative fills.

Before admission, compare that prediction with original audio: whole-gap error ≤5%, signed correlation ≥0.98, per-cycle error ≤8% and correlation ≥0.97, and no quiet block where the model predicts material. Quiet/endings, unrelated waveforms, inverted phase and unstable contexts are rejected in tests. Verification is not source separation; uncertainty remains attached to every inferred occurrence.

In this recording, 67 context-eligible proposals passed waveform verification: 51 single-period and 16 two-period continuations (83 inferred periods). Context-ineligible gaps remain untouched. No thresholds were tuned after observing the residual.

Coherence and uncertainty

B has slightly more residual energy than A but fewer modeled runs (201 versus 229), because it connects entire bounded gaps rather than adding only individual detected cycles. Some run breaks are tiny and may not be audible. B’s lower new-region error also reflects a different, more selectively supported set of intervals; it does not prove B is universally better. Of the newly modeled original ambiguous cycles, 76 overlap between branches, 13 are A-only and 7 are B-only. Those seven include five prior neighbor rejections and two prior periodicity-confidence rejections.

All original 4H accepted cycles and render-family choices remain. B uses new negative IDs and explicit sequence-inferred, not directly recognized provenance; identity labels are not merged. Unsupported material remains in residual. Inferred phases/amplitudes are predictions with recorded anchor IDs and verification metrics, not newly certified detections.

Waveform and discontinuity checks

New-region error is 1.720% for A and 1.328% for B. Worst new individual-period error is 2.331% in A and 1.605% in B. Maximum whole-waveform adjacent step remains 0.209021, identical to 4H. Maximum boundary step is 0.00563875 in A and 0.00563840 in B, versus 0.00563840 in baseline at the same evaluated boundaries. These statistics do not establish absence of audible clicks, gating or tonal artifacts.

Please assess naturalness by listening. No new listening was performed. The 64-position upper-harmonic limitation remains unchanged. Reconstruction + residual matches original within 7.45e-9 per sample for both variants; this is export/subtraction conservation, not complete sound recovery.
